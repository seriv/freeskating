## App name

Freeskate

## Short description

Records freeskating (JMKRIDE-style, independent pivoting footplates) as its
own activity type: GPS-based distance and speed, time-in-heart-rate-zone
using your configured zones, grade and grade-adjusted speed, and a lap
button.

## Description

Freeskate is a Connect IQ activity app for freeskating -- riding on a board
with independent pivoting footplates (JMKRIDE-style casters), not a
traditional skateboard or the step-based gait Garmin's built-in Walk/Run
profiles assume.

- GPS-based distance and speed (not step-derived)
- Time spent in each of your Garmin-configured heart-rate zones
- Smoothed grade (% slope) and a rider-tunable grade-adjusted speed, so
  effort on hills is comparable to effort on flats
- Manual and distance-based auto-lap
- Pause/resume with a deliberate two-step stop, so a recording can't be
  ended by one accidental button press
